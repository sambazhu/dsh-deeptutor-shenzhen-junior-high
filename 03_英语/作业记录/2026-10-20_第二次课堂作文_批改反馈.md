## 二、需要订正的问题（逐条）

| # | 原句 | 问题 | 改法 | 考点 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | My favourite classes is geography | 主谓不一致 | My favourite **class is** geography | 主谓一致（be 动词随主语） |
| 2 | after classes over | 句式错误（缺系动词/介词） | **After class is over** | 句子要有谓语 |
| 3 | I aways goto | 拼写 + 空格 | I **always go to** | always 拼写；单词间留空格 |
| 4 | It tell me | 主谓不一致（it 三单） | It **tells** me | 实义动词三单加 s |
| 5 | hear a different answer with you | 介词搭配 | hear a different answer **from** you | hear … from sb. |
| 6 | What about you. | 问句标点 | What about you**?** | 句号→问号 |
| 7 | It is a new class in junior high. | 选词 | It is a new **subject** in junior high school. | class=一节课；subject=学科 |
| 8 | I geography as it lets me … | 缺谓语动词 | I **love** geography as it lets me … | 句子缺动词 |
| 9 | 单词间距 / 长句逗号 / 署名 | 书写细节 | 词间留空格；长句适当加逗号；署名写 **Austin** | 卷面规范 |
