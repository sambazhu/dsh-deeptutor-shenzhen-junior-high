Passage 1    深圳迎来亚太盛会

【阅读原文】

     The 33rd APEC Economic Leaders’ Meeting is scheduled to take place in Shenzhen, a modern
city in southern China, on November 18–19, 2026. This high-level gathering is one of the most
important international events of the year.

     APEC stands for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation. Founded in 1989, it now has 21
member economies, including China, the United States, Japan, Australia, and Russia. Its main goal
is to promote free trade, economic growth, and regional cooperation among Asia-Pacific countries.

     Shenzhen, often called China’s “Silicon Valley,” has made great efforts to prepare for the
meeting. The city government has upgraded its airport, subway lines, and roads to make
transportation more convenient. Many hotels have been renovated to provide comfortable
accommodations. Over 5,000 volunteers, who can speak different languages, have been trained to
offer assistance. Security measures have also been strengthened to ensure a safe environment.

     During the two-day summit, leaders and representatives will discuss a wide range of issues.
The key topics include boosting the digital economy, tackling climate change, making supply
chains more stable, and encouraging inclusive growth. They will also explore ways to deepen
regional cooperation and solve common problems.

     Hosting the APEC meeting is a great honor for Shenzhen and a symbol of China’s growing
influence in world affairs. It gives the city a chance to showcase its achievements in innovation and
development. Moreover, it is expected to boost local tourism, attract foreign investment, and create
new business chances. Many experts believe that the outcomes of this meeting will shape the future
of the Asia-Pacific area and contribute to global peace and prosperity.

Passage 2     APEC 中国年即将开启

【阅读原文】

      Shenzhen to Host APEC 2026: A Symbol of China’s Opening-Up

      In a significant step for China’s diplomatic and economic engagement, the nation formally
assumed the role of APEC host in November 2025. The baton was passed during the meeting in
Gyeongju, South Korea, setting the stage for the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders’ Meeting to be
convened in Shenzhen, Guangdong, in November 2026. This marks China’s third time hosting the
prestigious event, cementing its proactive role in regional cooperation, with previous summits held
in Shanghai (2001) and Beijing (2014).

      Chinese authorities have highlighted the symbolic significance of choosing Shenzhen. Foreign
Ministry spokesperson Mao Ning described the city as both the “testing ground” for China’s
reform and opening-up policy and a vital “window” into its high-level opening to the world. She

expressed confidence that Shenzhen would present an exceptional event, with all parties
anticipating the “Shenzhen appointment.”

      The choice of Shenzhen is deeply symbolic. From a small fishing village to a global tech and

APEC 2026 is not merely about organizing a meeting; it is a strategic narrative. It offers China a
platform to showcase the tangible outcomes of its reform policies and to further champion regional
economic integration and an open world economy.

Passage 3     青年志愿者备战 APEC

【阅读原文】

      This November, a big city in southern China will hold an important international meeting —
APEC, a group of 21 economies. The whole city is preparing, and its young volunteers are getting
ready for the big day, too.

      To welcome guests from around the world, young volunteers are taking training classes, where
they practice foreign languages and learn how to behave politely at international events.

      This summer, more than 60 university volunteers served at an APEC meeting about forests.
Their job was to help with check-in and venue services, and everything went smoothly and quickly.
The guests praised them as professional, warm and efficient. In the “woodpecker” action, young
volunteers and international students look for mistakes on English signs around the city, with the
help of an AI tool. They have now reported more than 8,000 such problems, and the city has
improved over 40,000 public signs.

      Why do young people love volunteer service? For many, it is a chance to grow and build a
bridge between cultures. Small acts — a warm welcome, a corrected sign, a translated sentence —
help the world understand the city better. When the guests arrive in November, these young faces
will shine.

作文一       APEC 志愿者自荐信

【题目】

2026 深圳 APEC 招募青少年志愿者，你是李华，写一封自荐信，介绍自己的优势（英语能
力、沟通能力、对深圳的了解、志愿服务经历），表达自己想成为志愿者的愿望和承诺。

【范文】

Dear APEC Volunteer Recruitment Team,
     I’m Li Hua, a Grade 9 student from Shenzhen. I am writing with great enthusiasm to apply for
a volunteer position for APEC 2026 in our city, as I firmly believe that I have the qualities needed
to be an excellent volunteer.
     First, I have a good command of English, which enables me to communicate with foreign
guests fluently and accurately. Second, I am outgoing and easy-going, and I excel at communicating
with people from different cultural backgrounds, so it is easy for me to build good relationships
with visitors. Besides, having lived in Shenzhen for many years, I am familiar with its history,
technological achievements and green development, which allows me to introduce our city’s charm
to the world vividly. What’s more, I have taken part in various volunteer activities at school and in
the community, so I have rich experience in voluntary work.
      In short, if I am fortunate enough to be selected, I will spare no effort to help foreign guests,
strictly follow the rules and show the most positive image of Shenzhen teenagers. I would be
grateful for the opportunity and look forward to your reply.
                                                                                     Yours faithfully,
                                                                                               Li Hua

作文二        APEC 宣传稿

【题目】

为迎接 2026 年 APEC 会议在深圳举办，你校发起 “青春使者，讲述深圳——APEC 特别企
划” 活动，主题为 “科技绿洲，智慧深圳”，邀请同学们分享自己心目中的 “深圳科技名片”
和 “深圳生态名片”（名片可以是某个地方或某件物品）。假定你是校报英语编辑李华，请写
一篇英语宣传稿，向同学们简要介绍活动内容，推荐你心目中的这两张名片、说明理由，并
动员大家积极参与，争当“深圳故事讲述者”。参考词汇：名片 card；生态 ecology。

【范文】

     Hello, everyone! To welcome APEC 2026 in Shenzhen, our school is launching a special
activity, “Youth Ambassadors: Telling Shenzhen’s Stories”, under the theme “Tech Oasis, Smart

Shenzhen”. Students are invited to introduce what can best represent Shenzhen’s technology and
ecology. It is highly recommended that everyone take an active part in it.
      First, as for the tech card, drones undoubtedly come to my mind. What amazes me most is how
they combine technology with daily use. That is why I choose drones. Second, when it comes to the
ecology card, Shenzhen Bay Park is surely the best choice. This beautiful park not only offers
citizens a peaceful place to relax but also creates a safe home for many birds, showing perfect
harmony between the city and nature.
     What’s your pick? Join us! Let’s become proud ambassadors who bridge cultures and tell the
story of Shenzhen, making China’s voice heard worldwide. Small steps can make a big difference.
That’s all. Thank you!

作文三       APEC 介绍（APEC Theme and Shenzhen’s Practice）

【题目】

以 “APEC Theme and Shenzhen’s Practice” 为题，介绍 2026 深圳 APEC 的官方主题，以及深
圳在开放、创新、合作方面的实践。

【范文】

     Hello, everyone! The official theme of APEC 2026 in Shenzhen is “Building an Asia-Pacific
Community to Prosper Together,” which calls for openness, innovation and cooperation among all
Asia-Pacific economies.
     Shenzhen is putting this theme into practice in many ways. First, as a window into China’s
reform and opening-up, the city remains open to people and resources from all over the world.
Second, when it comes to innovation, not only does Shenzhen lead in high-tech fields such as 5G
and drones, but it also turns technological advances into everyday convenience. Besides, by
working closely with other cities in the Greater Bay Area, Shenzhen shows the spirit of cooperation
and contributes to shared development.
     In conclusion, it is Shenzhen’s practice that sets a good example for APEC, and it will surely
help achieve the goals of the theme. That’s all. Thank you!


【参考译文】
  大家好！2026 年深圳 APEC 的官方主题是“共建亚太共同体，共促繁荣”，它呼吁所有
亚太经济体秉持开放、创新与合作。
  深圳正在以多种方式践行这一主题。首先，作为中国改革开放的窗口，深圳始终向来自
世界各地的人才和资源敞开大门。其次，在创新方面，深圳不仅在 5G 和无人机等高科技领
域处于领先地位，而且还将技术进步转化为日常便利。此外，通过与粤港澳大湾区其他城市
密切合作，深圳体现了合作精神，并为共同发展作出贡献。
  总之，正是深圳的实践为 APEC 树立了良好榜样，它也必将有助于实现该主题的目
标。我的发言到此结束，谢谢大家！
